The Passion Behind the Collection

Our Story

DiNuzzo Garage is a private automotive collection in the heart of New York's Hudson Valley. Celebrating the beauty, engineering and history of remarkable automobiles, the collection brings together distinctive cars from a golden era of motoring.

From timeless European grand tourers to iconic American classics, every automobile has a story worth preserving.
