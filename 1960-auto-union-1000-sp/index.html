Auto Union 1000 SP

Sport Coupé

DiNuzzo Garage photography · atmospheric background

Nicknamed the baby Thunderbird for its distinctive proportions, the Auto Union 1000 SP pairs American-inspired styling with the unexpected engineering of a German two-stroke sports coupé.

An uncommon interpretation of the sports car

The 1000 SP was a rare, stylish member of the Auto Union family, with a low-slung body and dramatic detailing that contrasted with its front-wheel-drive, three-cylinder two-stroke underpinnings. Its unusual combination makes it a particularly memorable piece of midcentury motoring history.
